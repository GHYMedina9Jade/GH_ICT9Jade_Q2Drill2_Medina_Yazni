About Us

OB Montessori is an educational institution dedicated to providing quality education through the Montessori approach. It encourages students to become independent, responsible, confident, and lifelong learners.

Our Mission

To guide students in developing their abilities, values, and love for learning

Our Vision

To help develop responsible, independent, and confident individuals prepared for the future.

Our Educational Philosophy

OB Montessori follows the Montessori approach, which encourages hands-on learning, independence,discipline, and self-discovery.

Our History

OB Montessori was founded by Preciosa S. Soliven and has been providing Montessori education in the Philippines for generations.
